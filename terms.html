Core Breach: Zero - Terms of Service

Free to play, forever

Core Breach: Zero is free. There are no ads and no payments. Nexus Coins, Battle Tokens and everything else in the game are play currency with no real-world value and cannot be bought or sold.

Your data

Today the game collects no personal data beyond what you type to make a commander account (a commander name, an email address and a passcode). The passcode is stored only as a salted hash, never as plain text. Your progress is stored with your account.

The game is made by Loomis and hosted by Gamble Limited Ltd (GLL). When GLL hosts the game, GLL holds the accounts and their data. You can ask for a copy of your data, or for your account to be deleted, at any time (see Contact below).

Play fair

Do not cheat, use exploits or glitches to gain an unfair edge, attack or overload the servers, or harass other players. Accounts that break these rules may be reset or removed.

As is

The game is provided as it is, without promises. It may change, break, reset or go offline, and progress could be lost. Use it at your own risk.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/Gamble-Limited-Ltd/Core-Breach-Zero/issues for questions, bug reports, data export or deletion requests.
